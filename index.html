O(n²)Quadratic growth

Doubling the records can make simple comparison sorts take roughly four times as much work.

O(n log n)Efficient sorting

Merge, heap, and average-case quick sort handle large datasets more efficiently.

O(log n)Binary search

For 1,024 sorted IDs, binary search needs at most about 10 comparisons.

How to read this table

Big-O describes how work grows as the input grows. Actual timings also depend on implementation, hardware, and the data itself.
